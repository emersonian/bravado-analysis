The absence of QuickBooks sessions after April 8, 2026 therefore does not establish absence of accounting activity.

What the log does not say. QuickBooks' exported audit log records that an entry was edited, not which fields changed. Field-level history exists inside QuickBooks Online and would settle the question; it is not in the export. What can be established from the ledger: the entry's amount is $69,033.00, and the supporting document attached at creation was named "Jer Wages Accrual - $69033.pdf" — so the dollar figure at the end matches the dollar figure at the start. Whatever the three edits changed, they did not change the amount. Date, account coding, memo text, or classification remain possible.
