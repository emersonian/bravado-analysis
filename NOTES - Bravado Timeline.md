# Bravado Timeline — Draft 2

*(Draft 1 by Blake, Aug 25 2026; investment dates and ledger cross-references added from the books. Bracketed notes flag items where the ledger and the draft differ.)*

**2020 EOY** — Bravado's last profitable year, posting a $310k gain ($309,938 per the QuickBooks P&L export). Year-end total liabilities: $45,728.

**2021 EOY** — $116k loss ($116,154). Liabilities climb to $378,042.

**2022 EOY** — $31k loss ($30,966). Liabilities $502,261.

**2023-07-21** — Co-founder James Nelson leaves Bravado (50% owner) (journaled in QuickBooks as AJE-173, "to close member equity and draw upon member exit"). His QuickBooks access is cut October 13, 2023, the same day as sales lead Vince Blasco's.

**2023-12-29** — **Hugh & Christy Nguyen invest $200,000** — FEDWIRE into Chase, memo "OBI=EQUITY INVESTMENT," five months after James's exit was booked. Within eight days, ~$34,610 of it goes to Jer's personal cards and accounts.

**2023 EOY** — Bravado posts a loss of $565k ($564,654), its worst year ever. Liabilities $869,363.

**2024-04-22** — Hugh & Christy's $200k is reclassified as $100k loan + $100k equity (papered by CPA William S. Annon, months after the wire).

**2024-07-21/22** — **First Comatri investment agreement signed** (Company Jul 21, Investor Jul 22): $15,000 for a 3% membership interest, "payment has already been collected," effective July 22, with an option to invest up to $35,000 more for up to another 7% "by Oct 2, 2024 at this same valuation." The Company represents (§2) that it is duly registered, that "pursuant to the Company's Operating Agreement, all approvals have been acquired," that no legal action is pending, and that it is "current in all tax filings and there are no outstanding assessments or other liabilities." **Comatri's first investment: $15,010** lands in Chase savings the same day (via Coinbase ACH; the accountants book it to an equity account the bank feed names "Member Equity - Coinbase"). *(Ledger note: on that date the balance sheet showed ~$660,797 of total liabilities, including a $6,441 remnant of deferred 2020 employer Social Security tax whose CARES Act payment deadline was Dec 31, 2022 — see `ANALYSIS - First Comatri Agreement Reps` section of the forgiven-loan memo.)*

**2024-10-02** — Jer pitches Blake (the "To Blake Only" materials, including the purchase-order history).

**2024-11-26** — **Second Comatri investment agreement signed.** (The July agreement's $35,000 option had expired Oct 2, 2024; the December investment was made under this fresh agreement.)

**2024-12-02** — **Comatri's second investment: $35,000** (Coinbase ACH, six days after signing). Comatri total: **$50,010**. No member-equity account in either ledger ever carries Comatri's name.

**2024-?** — The last time a Texas LLC update was filed, still listing James Nelson as a member, not mentioning any new investors.

**2024 EOY** — Bravado posts a loss of $295k ($294,663). Liabilities $699,125.

**2025 EOY** — Bravado posts an operating loss of $272k ($271,832); net loss was $115k ($115,190) due to a one-time government subsidy, the $157k ERC ($156,642). Liabilities $805,512, of which $176,914 is wages accrued to Jer.

**2026-01/02** — Jer moves to NYC, away from his business's Houston headquarters. When pressed he does not give any coherent justification. He draws over $30k from the business in Jan/Feb 2026 (~$33.2k per the Finaloop ledger: ~$17.5k distributions + ~$15.8k unitemized card-bill payments), unexplained, nearly as much as his annual pay in recent years. *(Ledger note: Brooklyn personal transactions and NYC transit charges appear from March; the recurring NYC telehealth charges begin March 1. Blake's recollection of the move is Jan–Feb.)*

**2026-03-24 / 03-27** — Last Gusto payroll run; Strata Cloud Accountants' last activity in QuickBooks (their logged events fall 1,111 in January → 198 in February → 6 in March, then none). From mid-April the remaining team is paid by direct wires, off payroll.

**2026-04-16** — Jer tells Blake he no longer wants to run Bravado. He is burned out, does not want to run the company, and says he will resign in January.

**2026-04-29** — Jer asks Blake for an urgent one-month **personal loan of $7,500, to be repaid as $8,500**. The purpose presented is to pay down an SBA loan balance. The loan is never booked in either accounting system; it first appears in company records on July 20, 2026, added to the cash-plan debt schedule at $7,500 (not the agreed $8,500), already marked for deferral.

**2026-05-01** — A $4,600 "CAPITAL ONE - TRANSFER" lands in the Found account and is booked by Finaloop to **"Capital - Blake Benthall"** (Blake is verifying whether Comatri actually sent this). Finaloop also carries Comatri's $50,010 under that same personal-name capital account — not under Comatri.

**2026-07-21** — Jer shares the July 2026 investor update (the eight-page "State of the Business") privately with Blake for feedback before the investor meeting. Blake tells Jer it's okay to fail, that he needs to prioritize his mental health, and that Bravado should file for bankruptcy protection. In the same meeting Jer privately says he will be taking ~10 days away from Bravado and that his girlfriend Marissa will be running it; he shares Mark's and Marissa's contact information as the primary contacts while he's away.

**2026-07-22** — First LLC member update ever with all members present. Jer presents a different (shorter, v4) investor update to the members — telling Hugh & Christy for the first time that James left the company, saying it happened in 2024. Blake's recollection is that James left in 2023 (the books agree: the member-exit entry is dated July 21, 2023), and seeing other red flags in the investor update (large edits between the version shared with Blake vs. with other investors), Blake begins looking through Bravado's books. (Comatri has the contractual right to Bravado's financials.)

**2026-08-24** — Second investor update. Bravado missed projections by a wide margin; the update amends previously shared financial figures. When Blake asks who ran Bravado for the past 30 days, Jer asks Blake to leave the video call, and tells Blake that he will not be included in future member meetings.
