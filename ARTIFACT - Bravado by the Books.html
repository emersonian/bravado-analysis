EXHIBIT E

Proposed next steps

  1. Records request. Existing documents only: federal returns and K-1s, any Form 2553, the December 2025 Finaloop report, bank statements, written creditor agreements, and member consents.
  2. Tax classification. The form number on the members' K-1 packages — Form 1065 (partnership) or Form 1120-S (S corporation) — determines whether a corporate member is permissible.
  3. Defer commitments. No loan-resumption or deferral decisions until the requested records are received.
  4. Counsel review. The written statements in the investor documents, reviewed against the company's records.
