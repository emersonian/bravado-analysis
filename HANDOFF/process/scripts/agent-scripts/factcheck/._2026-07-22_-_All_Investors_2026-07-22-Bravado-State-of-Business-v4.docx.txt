2024: The year the wheels came off

-   My business partner's health crisis surfaced hidden, unvetted contracts written by his sales team. I I had to ask him to step down. He did so legally and willingly, though not without a lot of damage to the business, which I learned about after his departure.

-   The company was sized to support the business partner’s and sales teams’ plan.

-   Large contracts brought revenue but contained substantial chargebacks, slotting fees, and promotional commitments that crippled margin.

-   The Long tail of chargebacks, penalties, and financing fees dragged into mid-2025.

-   Result: $360K operating loss, $295K net loss, cash fell from $166K to $17K during the year.

2025: Triage, and it worked

-   Worked out of the bad contracts. Walked away from unprofitable accounts.

-   Cut the sales team. Impaired top-line growth but stopped the bleeding.

-   Invested in higher-margin sales channels: deeper HEB partnership, Faire market rhythm, focus on DTC: website and Amazon

-   Cut overhead nearly in half: rent $64.6K → $30.6K (subleased, then moved); total OpEx $591K → $354K.

-   Strong margin improvement across the year: full-year 28.7% → 32.4%, but the real story is the split , 1H 2025 was 24.7% (still absorbing legacy chargebacks); 2H 2025 jumped to 40.1% once the bad contracts washed out. That underlying margin profile carried into 2026 at 59.4% YTD. Volume we walked away from was not profitable volume.

-   The $155K Employee Retention Credit refund landed in 2025 and went to debt, inventory, and survival.

-   Underlying operating loss cut from $360K to $203K ex-ERC. But the buffer was gone: ended 2025 with $3,146 in the bank.

2026: Where we are + plan for the rest of the year

The shock (Q1-Q2):

-   Consumer discretionary spending dried up. Gas + grocery inflation, macro drag from the Iran/Strait of Hormuz situation. Specialty hot sauce is a discretionary food dollar.

-   Buc-ee's cut its entire hot sauce category to private label in April. Every brand in the set went. It removed one of our strongest retail accounts.

-   July 7, 2026: the operating account held $107. Low-water mark of a cash cycle, not a default

What is working , the turn:

-   Live 2026 gross margin: 59.4% YTD (74% product-only). First full year free of legacy chargeback drag from the old sales team's deals.

-   HEB via KeHE grew ~3x from strategic demo program. Confirmed KeHE POs in-hand: PO 3773648 (~$9,573 net Aug 10), PO 3802230 (~$9,358 net Aug 19), + Sep/Oct/Nov/Dec POs in the base plan.

-   Hot Shots wholesale reorder $11K confirmed Sep 15. Hot Sauce Bar Japan $2,726 (Sep 22). Dylan @ Heat Hot Sauce $2K (Sep 28).

-   Faire market cadence returned 6x on ad spend in Dec 2025. July 2026 Faire Market projecting $7K total, with $1,900 booked by EOD July 23.

-   Break-even honestly computed at ~$37K/month net cash. H2 plan averages $43K/month , comfortably above.
