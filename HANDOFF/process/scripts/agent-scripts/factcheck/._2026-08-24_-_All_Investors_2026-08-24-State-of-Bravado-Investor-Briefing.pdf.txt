6. Investor Reporting & Support
Proposed cadence: quarterly reporting, twice-yearly meetings
 •​ A healthy cash cycle runs 45-90 days from production to payout, and demand is seasonal. A single KeHE payout
    landing a week later moves a monthly number without anything in the business having changed, and any one month is
    either a peak or a trough rather than a trend. Monthly reporting on this cycle produces noise; quarterly reporting
    produces signal.
 •​ A quarter is also the unit management operates in: one production cycle, one selling season, one full receivables turn.
    Q3 closes Sep 30, Q4 closes Dec 31 - both land where a real decision gets made.
 •​ Proposal: a reporting pack within 30 days after each quarter close, and a live State of Bravado meeting twice a year -
    Jan 2027 and Aug 2027.
 •​ The cash plan is maintained continuously and reconciled with the bank; feel free to request the current file or call
    anytime.
