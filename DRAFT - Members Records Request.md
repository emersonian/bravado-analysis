We are writing together, as members holding 3,000 of the Company's 10,700 units, to request copies of the records listed below. We make this request under:

- **Operating Agreement §4.6**, which provides that upon request the Managers shall supply to any member information regarding the Company or its activities, and that each member may inspect and copy **all books, records and materials** in the Manager's possession;

- **Texas Business Organizations Code §101.502** [counsel to confirm citation], which entitles members to examine and copy company records on written request for a proper purpose.

**Our purpose** is proper and specific: to verify the financial information that has been provided to members in the July 22 and August 24, 2026 investor communications; to understand the status and tax treatment of our own membership interests and member loans; and to make an informed decision on the deferral and March 2027 repayment questions you have put to us. You asked us in July to hold you accountable — this is us doing that.

We have kept this request deliberately light. Every item in Sections A–D is an **existing document**: producing it means locating and forwarding a file, not creating anything new. Electronic copies (PDF or native files) by email or shared drive are fine. Where granting **read-only access** to a system (Finaloop, bank portal) is easier than exporting, we will accept access in lieu of copies. We are happy to receive documents on a rolling basis as they are located.
